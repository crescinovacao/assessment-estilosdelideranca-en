Leadership Styles

Based on research by Daniel Goleman in partnership with the consulting firm Hay/McBer

Discover your predominant leadership style and expand your repertoire to lead with more awareness and impact.

There is no ideal style. What defines effectiveness in leadership is understanding your predominant style and knowing how to switch between different approaches depending on the team, the moment and the context.

How to answer

For each statement, choose the number that best represents how often you act this way in your day-to-day as a leader.

Scale: 1 = Never  |  2 = Rarely  |  3 = Sometimes  |  4 = Often  |  5 = Always

There are no right or wrong answers. What matters is the honesty of your self-perception: answer based on what really happens, not on what you believe the ideal behavior would be.
